Your junta, without the notebook or the screenshots

Keeping count of who paid, chasing receipts and reminding late members still happens in notebooks and group chats.

How it works

Three steps, no spreadsheet.

  1. 1

    Create or join a junta

    The organizer sets the amount, the number of members, the schedule and the turn order. Everyone else joins with a code.

  2. 2

    Pay and upload the receipt

    Each member transfers straight to whoever collects this round, then uploads the receipt from their phone.

  3. 3

    Pozzo checks and keeps count

    Pozzo reads the receipt, compares it with what was expected, updates the pot and reminds anyone who is late.
