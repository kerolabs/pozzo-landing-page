Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Who is responsible

Kerolabs, a student startup from Peru, is the data controller for the information collected on this site. Contact: hello@kerolabs.example.

What we collect

Only what you type in the waitlist form: your name, an email address or mobile number, and your acceptance of these terms. Sharing it is voluntary. We do not ask for money, ID numbers or payment details.

How we use it

To send you one notice when the Pozzo app is available, and to answer you if you write to us. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Who processes it

The form data is received by the form provider configured for this site. If no provider is configured, the form opens your email app and the message travels through your own email service to Kerolabs.

Cookies and local storage

This site sets no cookies and has no analytics or tracking. The theme button saves your choice in your browser's local storage. That value stays on your device and is not sent to us or to anyone else.

How long we keep it

Until we send the launch notice or until you ask us to delete your data, whichever comes first.

Your rights

Under Peru's Ley N.º 29733 (Ley de Protección de Datos Personales) you can ask to access, correct or delete your data. Write to hello@kerolabs.example and tell us which right you want to use.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
